Set up the cups, then start the run. Switch lenses at any time: every lens reads the same experiment.

Protocol

  1. Carry the hole punch to a plant on the shelf and tap it: ten disks.
  2. Pick up a syringe, tap the pile of disks to load them.
  3. Tap the 0.2% NaHCO₃ bottle to draw 5 mL (the carbon source).
  4. Tap the detergent for one drop, so the water wets the leaf.
  5. Tap empty bench to pull a vacuum; repeat until every disk sinks.
  6. Tap a cup to pour the disks in.
  7. Drag the lamp head to set its height; slide a filter in if you want one.
  8. Tap the timer and count the disks that float each minute.

Nothing stops you doing it wrong. Skip the detergent or the vacuum and see what the data say.
